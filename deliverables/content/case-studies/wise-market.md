# Wise Market — AUD 40K to AUD 2.7M monthly revenue in six months

Source of record: `Case Study by Zeppstr.pdf` (Case Studies & Portfolio).
Client: Wise Market (wisemarket.com.au) · Australia · E-commerce, new and
refurbished technology.

## The situation

Wise Market sells new and refurbished consumer technology into the Australian
market — a category where margin is thin, price comparison is one tab away, and
the buyer's decision is made on a search results page rather than on your website.

Monthly revenue sat at AUD 40,000. Organic visibility was minimal in a category
where the highest-intent traffic is almost entirely search-led, and paid campaigns
were spending without a structure that made the spend defensible.

## The diagnosis

Two problems, and they were connected.

The site was not structured to earn the searches that actually convert. In
refurbished tech, buyers search by exact model and condition — "refurbished xiaomi
phones", "iphone 12 australia best price" — not by category. Without pages built to
answer those queries, the highest-intent demand in the category was invisible to us.

Meanwhile paid was carrying acquisition alone. When paid is the only channel, every
sale is rented. Cost floors rise, and there is no compounding asset underneath.

## What we did

- Executed a comprehensive SEO programme across on-page, off-page and technical.
- Built high-quality, keyword-led content mapped to model-and-condition search intent.
- Restructured site architecture and usability so those pages could rank and convert.
- Rebuilt paid campaigns around cost efficiency rather than raw volume.

The sequencing mattered: organic was built to take over the queries paid was
overpaying for, so the paid budget could move to genuinely incremental demand.

## What changed

Organic traffic rose substantially, with a large set of commercial-intent keywords
landing in top SERP positions:

| Keyword | Position | Monthly volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iphone 11 pro max | 2 | 10K – 100K |
| xiaomi mi 11 ultra refurbished | 3 | 1K – 2K |
| iphone 12 pro | 5 | 10K – 100K |
| iphone 12 australia best price | 6 | 1K – 3K |
| realme refurbished mobiles | 6 | 1K – 3K |
| iphone 12 prices | 6 | 1K – 10K |
| refurbished xiaomi phones | 9 | 500 – 900 |
| refurbished motorola phones | 10 | 10 – 100 |

Paid efficiency improved materially against the rebuilt campaign structure.

**Monthly revenue moved from AUD 40,000 to AUD 2.7 million within six months.**

## What this proves

A 67× revenue movement is not a campaign result. Nothing in a single channel moves
a business that far.

What moved was the structure underneath: search pages built to intercept the exact
queries a refurbished-tech buyer types, an architecture that let those pages rank,
and a paid account rebuilt to stop paying for demand the organic side could now win
for free. Each channel stopped competing with the others and started compounding.

That is the pattern — and it is transferable to any category where the buying
decision is made on a search results page.
