# Homatico — the website rebuilt, then the demand to fill it

Source of record: `Portfolio Zeppstr.pdf` (Case Studies & Portfolio), Client
Testimonials. Scope and outcome below are described by the client in their own
words; no figures are attributed beyond what they stated.

## The situation

Homatico came to Zeppstr with a website that was not carrying its weight. The
engagement began there — content, design, and the visual assets to support both —
and then extended outward into the channels that bring people to it.

The sequence mattered. There is no point driving traffic to a site that cannot
convert it, and no point perfecting a site nobody arrives at.

## The diagnosis

Two problems that most agencies treat as separate engagements, and that are
actually one.

The site was the constraint on conversion: content, structure and imagery all
needed rebuilding before any acquisition spend was justified. But once rebuilt, a
site with no organic visibility and no social presence is a brochure — it converts
the demand you already had and creates none.

So the work had to run in order: fix the destination, then build the routes to it.

## What we did

**Phase one — the website.**
Content creation, website design, and image and video selection, run as one
systematic process rather than three hand-offs. A dedicated team working to a
defined approach, with the client in the loop throughout.

**Phase two — the demand.**
Social media management, search engine optimisation, and presence across the
platforms Homatico's buyers actually use. The channels were built onto the
rebuilt site, not bolted onto the old one.

## What this proves

The website-first sequence is not a preference, it is arithmetic. Every visitor
sent to a site that does not convert is paid for twice — once to acquire, and
again in the margin of the sale that does not happen.

Rebuilding the destination before opening the channels meant the SEO and social
work compounded against a site that could hold the traffic. That is why the
client's report is growth in *inquiries and business*, rather than growth in
sessions.
