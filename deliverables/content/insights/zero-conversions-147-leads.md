# Zero conversions on the platform, 147 in the CRM: what a broken account actually looks like

In February, an account we inherited recorded zero conversions on Google Ads.

The CRM had 147.

The leads were real. People had filled in forms, the sales team had called them, some had visited the site. None of it reached the ad platform. And because Smart Bidding optimises against the conversions it can see, the algorithm spent the month optimising against nothing.

This is the most expensive failure in paid media, and it is almost invisible from the dashboard. Spend looks normal. Impressions look normal. The account appears to be working.

## What the money did instead

With no conversion signal, the bidding had nothing to steer toward. Seventy percent of that month's ₹6.5 lakh Google spend went to a single brand campaign paying ₹101 per click — ₹4.6 lakh on the one campaign least likely to produce an incremental buyer, because brand terms convert well on the metrics the platform *could* still see.

The account was not underfunded. It was uninstrumented. And the two look identical on a spend report.

## Why more budget makes it worse

The instinct when results disappoint is to increase spend or change creative. Both are wrong here, and the first is actively harmful.

If conversion tracking is broken, additional budget is routed by an algorithm working from corrupted feedback. You are not buying more results. You are buying more of whatever the broken signal happens to favour — which, in this case, was people already searching for the brand by name.

Every rupee added before the tracking is fixed compounds the misallocation.

## The sequence that worked

**Reactivate and instrument first.** Search was turned back on with conversion tracking rebuilt. Cost per lead fell 44% month on month — not because anyone wrote better ads, but because the algorithm could finally see which clicks became enquiries.

**Then prune.** Six underperforming campaigns and creatives were cut in parallel. The first genuine signal appeared.

**Then concentrate.** The top four creatives were scaled to 92% of spend. That month produced 948 enquiries in 31 days — the account's best on record.

**Then build the compounding asset.** An awareness pool reached 21.4 lakh people at ₹0.58 each, creating a 31.7 lakh retargeting audience whose expected cost per lead next quarter is ₹900–1,500, against a blended ₹2,348 for cold.

Three months, ₹39.7 lakh of media, 1,690 qualified enquiries at ₹2,348 each.

The order is the whole point. Every step depended on the one before it, and none of them would have worked in February.

## How to check your own account tonight

Pull platform-recorded conversions for last month. Pull leads from your CRM for the same period. Compare.

They will never match exactly — attribution windows, offline conversions and de-duplication all introduce drift. A 10–20% gap is normal.

A gap of 100% means your bidding algorithm is flying blind, and has been for as long as the gap has existed.

Nobody will raise this with you. The platform reports what it can see and calls it the truth. The agency reports the platform. The only person who can catch it is whoever bothers to compare two numbers that live in different systems.
